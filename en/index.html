The villa

The pool

A heated saltwater pool facing the sea. With its wooden sun deck, loungers and the tropical garden all around, it is the heart of the villa, from morning to sunset.

II

The gallery

A gallery of over 80 m² open to the tropical garden, with views of the ocean and the island of Dominica. Breakfast, reading in the shade, a nap in the hammock.

III

The cathedral living room

Beneath a soaring white wooden roof, the living room opens through tall louvred doors onto the gallery and the sea. Linen sofa, rattan pendants, terracotta tiles and found objects: a bright, cool and calming Creole interior.

IV

The long table

Under the gallery, a dining area for twelve. In the evening, the rattan lamps and candles light up long dinners facing the garden.

Marie-Galante, the island of a hundred windmills

Round as a galette and set in a turquoise sea, Marie-Galante has kept what the Caribbean has sometimes lost: uncrowded beaches, endless sugar cane fields and an authentic, gentle way of life. Here is what awaits you, a few minutes from the villa.

Plan your stay

Getting to Marie-Galante

By ferry from Pointe-à-Pitre to Grand-Bourg harbour, in about one hour. Or by small plane to the island's airfield. The villa is 3 km from town and the harbour.

Getting around

A rental car is recommended to enjoy the beaches and sights. The island is small: everything is less than 30 minutes away by car.

When to come

All year round. The dry season, from December to May, is the most popular. The Terre de Blues festival takes place every spring.
